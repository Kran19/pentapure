### Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)
```text
Pentapure is a complete, enterprise-grade ERP and CRM operational platform designed specifically for manufacturing, processing, and trading enterprises. Built for real-time factory floor and office collaboration, Pentapure unifies multi-stage inventory tracking, sales order fulfillment, dispatch logistics, petty cash accounting, and workforce attendance into one high-performance, role-based mobile application.

Whether you are managing raw material intake, tracking factory production runs, allocating delivery trucks, or reviewing daily cashier statements, Pentapure provides instant, synchronized visibility across your entire organization.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
KEY MODULES & CAPABILITIES
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. EXECUTIVE DASHBOARD & REAL-TIME ANALYTICS
• Live operational KPI oversight for business owners and plant heads.
• Real-time tracking of active purchase requests, pending sales orders, and unfulfilled dispatches.
• High-level cash flow and daily ledger balance summaries across all branches.

2. MULTI-STAGE INVENTORY & STOCK LEDGER
• Dynamic, ledger-based stock accounting (calculated dynamically as Net = IN - OUT).
• Segmented inventory stages: Raw Materials, Semi-Finished Goods, and Finished Products.
• Product threshold and stock-limit alerts to prevent manufacturing bottlenecks.
• Comprehensive warehouse and location tracking with FIFO deduction logic.

3. SALES ORDERS & CLIENT CRM
• Streamlined sales order creation for distributors, wholesalers, and B2B clients.
• Itemized pricing, grade specifications, packaging types, and due-date tracking.
• Instant order status updates from OPEN to DISPATCHED and COMPLETED.

4. DISPATCH & LOGISTICS MANAGEMENT
• Seamless handoff from Sales to physical warehouse fulfillment.
• Real-time transporter assignment, vehicle number recording, and driver details.
• Instant Lorry Receipt (LR) slip and Proof-of-Delivery (POD) image upload.
• Automated stock deduction upon gate dispatch.

5. CASHIER FINANCIAL MANAGEMENT & EXPENSE AUDIT
• Daily Cash IN (receipts) and Cash OUT (expenses) ledger management.
• Categorized expense logging (Petty Cash, Freight, Fuel, Maintenance, Daily Wages).
• Instant receipt and invoice camera capture with secure document storage.
• One-click downloadable PDF ledger statements with auto-merged expense bills.

6. WORKFORCE & ATTENDANCE MANAGEMENT
• Department-wise workforce directory and employee shift tracking.
• Fast daily headcount marking (Present, Absent, Half-Day, Overtime).
• Salary calculation support for daily-wage, monthly-wage, and per-hour workers.
• End-of-month salary adjustment and attendance export reports.

7. PROCUREMENT & PURCHASE REQUISITIONS
• On-the-floor purchase orders initiated directly by production supervisors.
• Direct admin approval workflow before procurement.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
ENTERPRISE SECURITY & ROLE-BASED ACCESS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Pentapure implements strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC). Team members only see the modules and data permissions assigned to their specific operational duties:
• Admin & Plant Managers (Full oversight)
• Sales Representatives (CRM & Orders)
• Dispatch Officers (Logistics & Fleet)
• Cashiers & Accountants (Petty Cash & Statements)
• HR & Attendance Supervisors (Workforce)
• Factory Floor Teams (Requisitions & Production)

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS & REQUIREMENTS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
• Platform: Android 7.0 (Nougat) and higher.
• Network: Requires active Internet connectivity (Wi-Fi or Mobile Data).
• Hardware: Camera access (optional for receipt and LR slip photo capture).
• Privacy: Enterprise data is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard TLS/SSL protocols.

For support, enterprise inquiries, or account activation, please contact your company administrator or reach out to support@pentapureadmin.in.
```
